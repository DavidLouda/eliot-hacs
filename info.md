# ElioT Energy Monitor

Monitor your ElioT energy devices from VISIONQ.CZ in Home Assistant.

## Features

- Multiple device support (ElioT CLASSIC LoRaWAN / NB-IoT, ElioT PRO)
- Energy Dashboard compatible
- High Rate (VT) and Low Rate (NT) tariff tracking
- Automatic updates every 30 minutes (configurable from 15 minutes to 24 hours)
- Battery state and battery estimate, signal strength, subscription expiry
- Re-authentication and diagnostics download
